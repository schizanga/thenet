Chiron (the wounded healer) recently returned to Aries (cardinal, fire) where it will stay until April 2027. 

Aries, the first sign of the zodiac, represents new beginnings, identity, the masculine, conflict, and trail-blazing. Chiron is a smol dwarf planet and/or asteroid that orbits the Sun from space.
